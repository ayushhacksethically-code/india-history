# Corrections Log

This file records corrections made to the site after publication.
Each entry lists the page, the original claim, the correction, and
the date. Corrections are made publicly so readers can see what
changed and why.

## 2026-10-04 — Phase A corrections

### origins.html — Koldihwa dating
- Original: Koldihwa Neolithic dated c. 7000–3000 BCE.
- Corrected to: c. 2500–1500 BCE, with a note that the 7th-
  millennium BCE dates (Sharma 1980) were rejected after
  radiocarbon recalibration.
- Reason: The 1980 dates are no longer accepted by mainstream
  scholarship (Possehl 1999; Fuller 2006).

### dynasties.html — Chola village council attribution
- Original: The Uttaramerur inscription attributed to Rajaraja
  Chola I; described as "democratic."
- Corrected to: Attributed to Parantaka I (907–955 CE); described
  as "qualified lot-based," not democratic in the modern sense.
- Reason: Factual attribution correction and anachronism removal.

### freedom-struggle.html — Partition death toll
- Original: "1 to 2 million perished."
- Corrected to: Range of 200,000 to 2,000,000, with sources.
- Reason: Academic estimates vary widely; range more accurate.

### colonial-india.html — Colonial drain figure
- Original: "$45 trillion" presented as a single fact.
- Corrected to: Patnaik's compound-interest methodology
  disclosed, with Tirthankar Roy's critique noted.
- Reason: Methodology is contested; single figure misleading.

### index.html — Hero inconsistency
- Original: "Nine Millennia of Settled Life" vs "five millennia
  of continuous subcontinental history" without clarification.
- Corrected to: A clarifying note distinguishing settled village
  life (c. 7000 BCE onward) from recorded civilizational
  narrative (c. 2600 BCE onward).
- Reason: Remove apparent contradiction.

### js/reveal.js — Tall-element reveal bug
- Original: IntersectionObserver threshold 0.12 caused tall
  sections (glossary, 5,938px) to remain invisible at opacity 0.
- Corrected to: Dynamic per-element threshold — 0.12 for small
  elements, 0 for elements taller than 50% of viewport.
- Reason: Restoration of scroll-reveal animation for tall sections.

## 2026-10-11 — Phase C·3 to C·7 corrections

### origins.html — IIPA "first urban civilization" claim
- Original (from IIPA source): "The Indus Valley Civilization
  was the first known urban civilization."
- Corrected to: "One of the ancient world's earliest urban
  civilizations (Mesopotamia and Egypt predate Indus city-
  building)."
- Reason: Mesopotamian cities (Uruk, c. 3500 BCE) and Egyptian
  cities (c. 3100 BCE) predate the Mature Harappan phase
  (c. 2600 BCE).

### origins.html — Pashupati seal attribution
- Original (from IIPA source): "Pashupati is the name of the
  deity."
- Corrected to: "Identified as 'Pashupati' by Sir John Marshall
  (1931). The identification is contested by modern scholars
  (Doris Srinivasan, Audrey Truschke, others) — the figure may
  represent a shaman, a buffalo-horned deity, a yogic prototype,
  or a generic nature spirit."
- Reason: The "Pashupati" label is a colonial-era interpretation
  and is not accepted as settled fact by modern archaeology.

### dynasties.html — Ghaznavid & Ghurid dynasty added
- Original: Ghaznavid and Ghurid raids were absent from the
  page. Delhi Sultanate appeared to begin with the Mamluk
  dynasty, without its Turkic precursors.
- Corrected to: New "Ghaznavid & Ghurid Raids (1000–1206 CE)"
  dynasty card added with Mahmud of Ghazni (primary),
  Muhammad Ghori, Subuktigin, Muhammad Bakhtiyar Khalji
  (secondary).
- Reason: These campaigns preceded and enabled the Delhi
  Sultanate. Their omission obscured historical sequence.

### dynasties.html — Sikh Persecution entry added
- Original: Mughal–Sikh conflict was not represented in the
  Mughal dynasty card.
- Corrected to: New "Sikh Persecution under the Mughals

  Fateh Singh, and Banda Singh Bahadur.
